# Rift Runners

A free browser arena prototype with a trophy road, smarter practice bots, and experimental peer-to-peer rooms.

## Progression
- Win a bot match: +10 trophies
- Lose a bot match: -7 trophies, minimum 0
- Trophy cap: 80,000
- Wins, losses, trophies and personal best are stored locally in this browser.

## Controls
- Mobile: left virtual stick to move; BLAST to shoot; DASH to evade.
- Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to shoot, Shift to dash.
- Bot matches have four opponents that pursue, strafe, aim, dodge nearby projectiles and dash at close range.

## Online prototype
Create a room and share the code, or enter a friend's code. Peer-to-peer support is experimental and may not work on every network. Ranked trophy progression currently applies to bot matches; online match outcomes are not yet a secure competitive ranking system.

## Notes
This is an early prototype, not a full Brawl Stars equivalent. Progress is saved in the current browser/device and is not synchronized across devices.
